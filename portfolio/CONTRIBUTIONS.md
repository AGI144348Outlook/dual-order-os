# Contributions and scope

## Timothy's role

Product direction and evolving requirements: persistent source records; distinct identities retained through edits and links; reusable components; contextual persona filtering; Android as an important control surface; and observable evidence rather than fabricated experiment dialogue. Recent project records attribute these choices and corrections to Timothy.

AI development assistants contribute code, implementation proposals, documentation, tests and technical reviews. The published artifacts show the combined result. They do not independently establish unaided expertise in every language, security decision or research method.

- Persona extraction distinguishes name occurrences, selected branches, candidate session continuity and human approval.
