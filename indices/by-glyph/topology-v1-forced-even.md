# Topology v1 — Forced-Even 3-Root Clusters

**Assembly, not canon.** One of three unreconciled root-glyph assemblies over the same
underlying matrices — see `../README.md` for why all three are kept side by side rather than
picking one. This file transcribes the pairings from
`the-unicorn-estate/topology/01-glyph-topology-draft.md`, the *first-pass* draft: every one of
the 22 glyphs is forced to exactly 3 Latin roots, regardless of whether the underlying concept
is broad or narrow. It establishes the pictograph-as-anchor pairing method that both later
drafts (v2, v3) keep.

Root identifiers below are given in the classical stem form used by this source document
(e.g. `duc-`), which does **not** literally match the dictionary-word rows in
`matrices/latin-roots/roots.json` (extracted from a different, later source document — see
`matrices/latin-roots/README.md`). Treat this file's roots as historical/conceptual pointers
into the same semantic territory, not as row-keys you can join directly. Glyph names below do
match `matrices/hebrew-glyphs/glyphs.json` (`name` field) exactly.

## What the source document also observed

Building this forced-even pass, three complementary pairs and three rough functional families
fell out on their own (not planned going in):

- **Complementary pairs:** Gimel/Dalet (move/gate), Vav/Zayin (connect/sever),
  Mem/Samekh (flow/stabilize), Nun/Shin (generate/consume), Hey/Ayin (perceive/understand).
- **Functional families:** *structural* (Bet, Dalet, Het, Vav, Zayin, Tet), *agentive*
  (Yod, Kaf, Lamed, Ayin, Tsade, Resh, Shin), *world-level* (Aleph, Gimel, Hey, Mem, Nun,
  Samekh, Pey, Qof, Tav).

The source flags both of these as possibly-real structure or possibly an artifact of the
forced-even method — not confirmed either way. See `matrices/hebrew-glyphs/glyphs.json` for
each glyph's full proposed execution role, and `../README.md` for the open questions that
still apply to this document as a whole.
